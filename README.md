## Projects
### Projecting Bat Speed in MLB
In this work, I designed a projection model to forecast a player's bat speed. This involved a variety of components given the limited dataset that ranges from July 2023 to the present (August 2026 at time of work). To extend this dataset, I modeled the relationship between a player's latent 90th percentile exit velocity and a player's observed bat speed. Essentially, I learned the correlation between these two features and then via a multivariate approach, imputed for the missing years back to 2016. To obtain a player's latent exit velocity, I utilized a error measurement model via a random walk component. Conceptually, the observed exit velocity is noisy and the random walk learns the a player's underlying talent. With those components all specified, I forecast a player's bat speed using a Hilbert-Space Gaussian Process (HSGP) to learn a smooth aging curve, layering in hierarchical player random effects.

### Evaluating NBA Shot Skill
This is a two stage model. The first stage models a shot's probability based on its geometry, distance and angle. The second stage adds hierarchical components for shot type (Jumper, Dunk, Hook, and Layup) and learns player skill. In the first iteration, v1, we modeled the first stage with Bayesian Additive Regression Trees and in the second stage, we also included defensive and team effects. In the second iteration, v2, we extended our dataset to 17 seasons and modeled shot probability with an XGBoost model and in the second stage, modeled a player's skill in each shot type, while also learning the correlation between them. In essence, we learned how much a player's Dunk skill correlates to their Jumper skill. We then added on aging curves to project a player's skill into the future.
